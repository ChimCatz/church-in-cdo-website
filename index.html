Christ is our life, the church is our living

We are a local church in Cagayan de Oro City, gathering simply in the name of the Lord Jesus to know Him, love Him, and grow in Him together.

Welcome

Whether you are visiting Cagayan de Oro or you already call it home, we welcome you to come and see. Our meetings are simple: the reading of God's word, prayer, singing, and the fellowship of the saints, all centered on Christ.
