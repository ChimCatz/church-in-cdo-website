About Us

Who we are and what we practice as a local church.

Our Vision

We are a group of believers in the Lord Jesus Christ living in Cagayan de Oro City. We do not belong to any denomination; we simply meet as the church in this locality, following the pattern of the early believers in the book of Acts — taking Christ as our life and the church as our living.

What We Practice

Christ as Life

We believe that Christian life is not a matter of rules to keep, but Christ Himself lived out in us as our life and person.

The Church as the Body

We gather as one Body in a locality, without denominational names or divisions, according to the New Testament pattern.

The Word of God

Our meetings center on the plain reading and enjoying of the Bible, God's holy Word, as the ground of our fellowship.

Simple Gatherings

We meet in homes and simple venues for the Lord's table, prayer, hymns, and the fellowship of the saints.

Our History Here

The church in Cagayan de Oro is part of the recovery of the proper church life that the Lord has been raising up in many localities and nations. Here in CDO, saints gather together to pursue the Lord and to build up genuine fellowship among believers in this city.
