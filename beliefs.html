What We Believe

Our faith is founded on the holy Scriptures, common to all genuine believers in Christ.

The Holy Scriptures

We believe the Bible, comprised of the Old and New Testaments, is the complete and verbally inspired Word of God, and is the unique and final authority for faith and living.

The Triune God

We believe in the one true God, eternally existing as Father, Son, and Holy Spirit — three-one, coequal, and co-existing, distinct yet inseparable.

The Lord Jesus Christ

We believe that Jesus Christ is the eternal Son of God who became flesh, lived a perfect human life, died on the cross for our sins, resurrected, and is now the life-giving Spirit dwelling in every believer.

Salvation

We believe salvation is by God's grace through faith in the Lord Jesus Christ, not by works, and that all who call on His name are regenerated and receive eternal life.

The Church

We believe the church is the Body of Christ, composed of all believers, expressed practically in local churches — one church for each city or locality, without denomination or division.

The Lord's Return

We believe in the personal, bodily return of the Lord Jesus Christ, and we live in expectation of His coming.
